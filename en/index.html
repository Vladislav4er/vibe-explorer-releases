Features

A different set of windows for each task

Keep project windows, messages, and tools in separate groups. Arrange them across your displays and return to saved groups after a restart.

Window groups

Hold Shift and drag one window onto another. Name your group, choose a color, and rearrange its tabs.

Two windows in one tab

Put two windows in a split tab with its own layout and proportions. Switch between the pair and your regular tabs.

Previews and appearance

Hover over a tab to preview its window. Choose a light or dark tab bar, or let it match the color of the active window.

Installation

Start with two windows

  1. Install the app

    Unzip the download and drag Vibe Explorer.app into your Applications folder.

  2. Allow window management

    On first launch, enable vibe-explorer in System Settings under Privacy & Security → Accessibility. For window previews and dynamic colors, grant screen recording permission in the app’s settings.

  3. Create your first group

    Open two windows. Hold Shift and drag one onto the other.
