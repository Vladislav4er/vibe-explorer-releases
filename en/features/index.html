Groups and tabs

Turn two windows into a group

Hold Shift and drag a window onto another window or a group’s tab bar. Use the plus button to add windows. Rearrange tabs, move them between groups, or detach them. You can also lock a group’s membership.

Ungrouped windows

⇧ Shift

Hold Shift and drag an ungrouped window, or click Group Windows.

When you have many tabs

Scroll the tab bar or choose All Tabs from its context menu. The bar scrolls automatically as you drag toward an edge. Click a tab’s close button to remove the window from its group and minimize it. You can change the grouping key and delay in Settings.

Split view

Two windows in one tab

Put windows side by side or stack them vertically. Swap them, replace one, or move the divider. The split keeps its proportions and orientation when you switch to a regular tab and back.

Drag the divider. Switch to the Browser tab and back to see the split layout stay in place.

Window size

One size for every tab

Drag the corner to resize the group, then switch tabs. Each window takes the same space. Double-click an empty part of the tab bar to maximize the group within the desktop, then double-click again to restore its previous size.

Appearance

A tab bar that matches your window

The dynamic theme matches the tab bar to the active window’s title bar. You can also choose Light, Dark, or System and adjust tab width, bar height, and font size.

Hover previews and the dynamic theme use the macOS Screen Recording permission.

“Notes” preview
More appearance settings

In Settings, you can change the bar’s opacity, adjust the font size and weight, and show or hide icons. Use a tab’s context menu to set its color and a custom label for tooltips and menus.

Search and shortcuts

Find the right window with the keyboard

Press ⌃⌥S and type the name of a window, app, or group. Use the arrow keys to select a result and press Enter to open it. Use ⌃⌥← and ⌃⌥→ to switch tabs in the current group.

Workspace

Take a group to any display

In Mission Control, drag a visible group window to another regular desktop. The other available windows follow it. Use the group menu to move the group to a different display.

Both panes of a split stay visible in Mission Control. New app windows also move to the group’s display when Vibe Explorer can identify the right destination.

Work groups

Pick up where you left off

Open the group list from the macOS menu bar. Vibe Explorer saves group membership and appearance automatically. On relaunch, it reconnects the open windows it can identify. Closed windows stay listed as waiting.
